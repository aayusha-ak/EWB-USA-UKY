Students

Engineers Without Borders at the University of Kentucky gives students the opportunity to develop leadership, technical, and professional skills while making a positive impact through engineering and community service. Throughout the year, members participate in local volunterring activities, fundraising, outreach events, professional development, and future engineering initiatives.

We welcome students from every major. Our work benefits from a wide mix of backgrounds and perspectives, and anyone with an interest in contributing is encouraged to get involved. No previous experience is needed!




Support


A gift to Engineers Without Borders at the University of Kentucky advances practical, community-centered engineering solutions in regions facing urgent challenges. your support enables student teams to implement resilient infrastructure, expand access to clean water and energy, and build local capacity through collaborative partnerships. funding helps projects move through essential stages from design and materials to testing and training, ensuring that innovative concepts become sustainable and long-lasting systems.
