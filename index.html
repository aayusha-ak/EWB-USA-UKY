Students

We are currently working on a water systems project in Ecuador, where we handle everything from fundraising to technical design and project management. We typically meet for an hour each week to work on project tasks, with occasional work that takes place outside of meetings.

We welcome students from every major. Our work benefits from a wide mix of backgrounds and perspectives, and anyone with an interest in contributing is encouraged to get involved. No previous experience is needed!

Chemical, Environmental, and Biosystems Engineering — Think about water, materials, and environmental challenges in ways that connect your coursework to real community needs.

Civil and Mechanical Engineering — Gain experience approaching infrastructure‑style problems with real constraints and teamwork.

Biomedical and Human‑Centered Disciplines — Offer a people‑centered viewpoint that helps teams design solutions with community well‑being in mind.

Computer Science / Computer Engineering - Support data collection, analysis, and visualization to guide engineering decisions and communicate project impact.

Business & Finance — Support project sustainability by helping with budgeting, planning, fundraising strategy, and long‑term organizational decisions.

Marketing & Communications — Strengthen outreach by creating clear, engaging messaging that helps the chapter connect with communities, donors, and new members.

Spanish & Other Language Majors — Improve communication and cultural understanding when working with partner communities.


Professionals

We are always looking for additional mentors to support our work!

As a student‑led chapter, we are still building our technical experience. EWB‑USA requires all student work to be reviewed by both our Responsible Engineer in Charge (REIC) and an EWB‑USA engineer who specializes in evaluating chapter submissions. Beyond that, we actively seek second opinions from experienced mentors throughout the design process. Each trip to our partner communities also requires a mentor to accompany the team.

What we look for in mentors and how they support us:
  • Experience with water, sanitation, outreach, fundraising, or Spanish language skills
  • Offering guidance or second opinions as needed
  • Joining meetings or traveling with the team if desired
